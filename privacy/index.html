Privacy Policy for yafo

Last updated: November 2026

yafo ("yet another family organizer") is a family organizer for iPhone and iPad with a calendar, a shopping list and to-dos. The app keeps everything on your device and in your iCloud. The developer runs no server, there is no account of its own, and the developer receives none of your data.

In short

  • yafo collects no personal data for the developer and shares none with third parties.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no crash reports sent to the developer.
  • What you enter stays on your devices and in iCloud with Apple – shared only with the people you invite to a family.

What yafo stores

  • families and their members: names, initials and child profiles that you create,
  • calendars and events: title, time, place, notes, participants, repetition, comments and birthdays,
  • shopping lists and to-dos with quantities, due dates and assignees,
  • your settings, such as colors, reminder times, public and school holidays and subscribed calendar addresses.

This data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it from the device. Under Family → "Leave or Delete" you can also delete single families or all your data from iCloud.

iCloud and sharing with your family

yafo syncs through Apple CloudKit. Your own data and the families you created are stored in your private iCloud database. When you invite someone to a family – by link or QR code – iCloud shares that family with that person. All members of a family then see its calendars, events, comments, shopping list and to-dos. What you enter under "Just me" stays private.

Apple stores the data encrypted. The developer has no access to it. Apple's iCloud privacy terms apply. Without an iCloud sign-in, yafo works only locally on the device.

Backup and export

Once a week yafo saves a backup file in the yafo folder of your iCloud Drive (the last five; without iCloud, locally on the device). Exports as ICS, CSV or backup file are only created when you start them, and only go where you share them.

Holidays, school holidays and calendar subscriptions

  • Public holidays are calculated on the device, without internet access.
  • When you turn on school holidays for a calendar, yafo downloads them from the OpenHolidays API (https://openholidaysapi.org). Only the selected country, state or canton is sent.
  • Calendar addresses (ICS/webcal) that you enter yourself are fetched directly from that provider, for example Google.

As with any website request, these providers technically receive your IP address and the time of the request. Their privacy policies apply. yafo sends no information about you or your family.

Places

When you enter a place for an event, yafo suggests matching places using Apple Maps (MapKit). The text you type is sent to Apple for this. For places it finds, yafo stores the coordinates with the event.

Event from Screenshot

Pictures you open with "Event from Screenshot" or share to yafo are read exclusively on the device – with Apple's text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence on the device. The picture is neither stored nor uploaded; only the events you confirm are added.

Access on your device

yafo asks for calendars, contacts and the camera only when you use the feature, and for notifications on first launch. You can revoke them at any time in the device settings.

  • Calendars: show events from your device calendars in grey, import them once, or copy yafo events into a device calendar. Device calendars are not copied into yafo's iCloud data unless you import events.
  • Contacts: read names and birthdays to import them into a calendar. Only the birthdays you select are added.
  • Camera: scan the QR code of a family invitation. No pictures are stored.
  • Notifications: reminders for events and to-dos. They are scheduled on the device, not sent from a server.

Widgets and share menu

The widgets and the share menu extension read and write an excerpt of your data in a protected storage area that only yafo and its extensions can use.

Children

yafo is made for families. Child profiles are created by parents; the child needs neither a device nor an account. Their data is stored in the family's iCloud like everything else. A child with their own device joins by invitation with their own Apple Account.
