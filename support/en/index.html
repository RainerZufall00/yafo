Frequently asked questions

How do I invite my family?

Tap the family icon at the top right and choose “Invite with QR Code” or “Invite Members”. Each QR code works for exactly one person. The other person scans it in yafo (“+” › “Join with QR Code”) or with the Camera app and then sets up their own profile.

Does my child need their own device?

No. Create a child profile under Family – your child can then take part in events and be assigned to-dos. If they get their own iPhone later, invite them as a child; they take over their profile when joining.

Why don’t the others see my changes?

yafo syncs through iCloud. Check that every device is signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is turned on. Changes made in widgets are applied the next time you open the app.

How do I bring over my Google Calendar?

Under Family › “Import Events”: device calendars, an ICS address (in Google: “Secret address in iCal format”) or an .ics file. You can also subscribe to an ICS address in a calendar’s settings; yafo refreshes it whenever you open the app.

How does “Event from Screenshot” work?

Share a picture to yafo, or long-press “+” in the calendar and choose “Event from Screenshot”. yafo picks out date, time, place and repetition on your device and shows a preview to adjust. With Apple Intelligence, recognition is more accurate.

How do I back up my data?

yafo automatically saves a backup to your iCloud Drive every week (folder “yafo”). Under Family › “Export & Backup” you can share or restore a backup at any time and export events as ICS and lists as CSV.

Is my data stored anywhere else?

No. yafo has no server of its own and no account. More in the privacy policy.
